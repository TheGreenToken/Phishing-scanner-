// FAQ
What does AntiSec Phishing Scanner do?
It scans any public website for phishing indicators — suspicious login forms, fake banking pages, Discord webhooks, and other patterns used by attackers.
How accurate is the AI report?
The Groq AI analyzes the page content and provides a detailed risk assessment. It's highly effective but should not be the only factor in your decision.
What happens to the URLs I scan?
Nothing. Everything runs through our secure API. No data is stored or logged.
